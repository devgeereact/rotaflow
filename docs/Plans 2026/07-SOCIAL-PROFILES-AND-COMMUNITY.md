# 07. Brand name, social profiles and community

These are review-ready drafts. No account was created, checked by sign-up or published. Every handle below must be confirmed in each platform's own sign-up flow.

## 1. Settle the name first (decision D1)

Research on 10 October 2026 found (sources in the research notes, each seen that day):

| Finding                                                                                                                                                               | Severity                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| An iPhone app called **RotaFlow**, "Shift Calendar & Rota Planner for iPhone", owns `rotaflow.app` (VERIFIED by fetching the page title)                              | High: same category, same name                             |
| **FlowRota**, a UK B2B rota tool with GPS clock-in, timesheets, swaps and leave, listed on the App Store and Capterra UK                                              | High: same features, mirrored name                         |
| Companies House lists several "Rotaflow" companies, including ROTAFLOW FV LIMITED (rotary unions, owns `rotaflow.com`) and ROTAFLOW GLOBAL LTD (2023)                 | Medium: different trades, but they own the obvious domains |
| `@rotaflow` is taken on Instagram (1,034 followers, owner unknown), Facebook (a fire-protection firm in Canada) and LinkedIn (Rotaflow Fire Systems, 8,913 followers) | Medium: forces a suffixed handle                           |
| A US trade mark "ROTAFLOW" exists for blood pumps (class 10)                                                                                                          | Low: different class                                       |
| UK and EU trade mark registers                                                                                                                                        | **NOT TESTED**. Needs a direct search                      |

**Recommendation.**

1. Search the UKIPO register for "RotaFlow" and close variants in classes 9 (software) and 42 (software as a service). This is free on the UKIPO website and takes minutes.
2. If a live mark in class 9 or 42 conflicts, rename **before** spending on launch. Renaming now costs a domain, a logo pass and a find-and-replace. Renaming after launch costs search ranking, printed material and customer confusion.
3. If the search is clear, keep RotaFlow, consider filing a UK trade mark in classes 9 and 42 (a modest fee; confirm current fees on gov.uk), and use one consistent suffixed handle everywhere.

The profiles below assume RotaFlow stays. If the name changes, they need only the name and handle swapped.

**Bio (under 80 characters):**

**Format:** a phone screen recording, a real human voice, captions on, one problem per clip, 20 to 40 seconds. Synthetic names and places only.

**First series:** "Where's my shift?" (staff finds the next shift in 3 seconds) · "No signal in the basement" (a clock-in that waits and sends) · "Swap without the group chat" · "Publishing the week" · "Spot the gap before Saturday".

## 8. WhatsApp Community

For pilot and early-access customers only. Not a public channel.

Every community gets an announcement group where only admins post ([WhatsApp help](https://faq.whatsapp.com/768544097924262)). WhatsApp's help pages conflict on whether the Business app can create a community, so test this on the device first. Member phone numbers are visible to admins and to members of groups they join, and an @mention in the announcement group exposes that member's number to the whole community. Tell people this before they join.

**Name:** RotaFlow Early Access

**Description:**

> Product updates and feedback for RotaFlow early-access teams. Please don't share staff names, rotas, pay or health details here. For help with your account, use Help in the app or email support@rotaflow.space.

**Groups:** Announcements (admins only), and one Feedback group. No more until there is demand.

**Welcome message:**

> Welcome to RotaFlow Early Access. We'll share updates here first and ask for your views on what we build next. Keep questions general and remove names from screenshots. For anything about your own account, use Help in the app. This group isn't monitored around the clock and isn't for emergencies.

**Rules:** opt-in by invite link only, never bulk-add from a staff list, named owner plus a backup, remove personal data quickly, state moderation hours.

## 10. Safeguards and measurement

Separate support from marketing messages. Never ask for staff details in comments or DMs. Get written permission for any face, name, logo or quote. Measure qualified enquiries, demos and trials from each channel through the campaign tags. Followers and views are secondary. No paid ads, automated cross-posting or bulk invitations are authorised by this plan.
